ProAssist Support

Work calendar and earnings tracker for handymen and contractors.

Need help or have an idea? We read every message.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, with an automatic copy in your own iCloud (a private app folder only you can access). ProAssist has no servers of its own and never sees your projects, clients or photos.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Nothing to do: sign in to the same Apple ID on the new iPhone and install ProAssist. Your projects, settings and photos come back from iCloud automatically. You can also check Me → Backup → Restore from iCloud.

Without iCloud, open Me → Backup → Save backup and save the file anywhere; on the new iPhone choose Restore from file. A backup file does not include photos.

How is a project marked "profitable"?

ProAssist divides the project's profit (income minus expenses) by the hours you logged and compares it with the target hourly rate you set in Me → Taxes and rates.

How do I send an invoice or a quote?

Open a project and tap Invoice, or open an estimate and tap Send a quote. ProAssist creates a PDF and opens the share sheet so you can send it by Messages, Mail or any other app.

How do I delete my account?

Open Me → Account → Delete account. This permanently erases all data in the app and its iCloud copy. An active subscription is managed by Apple: cancel it in App Store → your account → Subscriptions.
